The Bre Collection Explained: Bre Tiesi's Three California-Designed Mattresses (2026)

The Bre Collection launched on November 5, 2026, with three mattresses designed in California and made-to-order in the USA by Furniture of America. The founder is Bre Tiesi, the television personality known from Netflix's Selling Sunset, and the brand's pitch is California living translated into sleep: Golden Hour, Sunset Glow and Midnight Diamond, priced from $999, $1,599 and $2,499. All three are hybrids with cooling gel and pocketed coils, and all three are sold only through the brand's own website. But the launch is four days old, no independent testing exists, and the brand has not published a sleep trial, a warranty or per-size prices. Here is what is actually known about each model — and what is not.

Quick answer

The short answer: The Bre Collection's three mattresses — Golden Hour from $999, Sunset Glow from $1,599 and Midnight Diamond from $2,499 — are 12-, 14- and 16-inch hybrids designed in California, made-to-order by Furniture of America, and sold only on the brand's own website. All three use PermaCold Cooling Gel, SmartFoam HD and pocketed OrthoCoils, with the plusher Euro tops and a natural latex layer as you climb the range. No sleep trial, warranty, shipping terms or per-size prices have been published, and no independent testing exists yet.

At a Glance: The Bre Collection

The Bre Collection is a celebrity-founded mattress line in the classic sense: Bre Tiesi, known from Netflix's Selling Sunset, has put her name on three mattresses built by Furniture of America, with the pitch framed around California living — golden mornings, ocean air, evenings fading into luxury. The brand's philosophy, 'Designed for the Life You Love,' is printed across the launch materials, and the three models carry the theme in their names: Golden Hour, Sunset Glow and Midnight Diamond.

  • Golden Hour — from $999: the 12-inch tight top and the most balanced feel of the three. Quilted gel memory foam, plush and medium SmartFoam HD layers, OrthoCoils pocketed coils and 360° edge support. The entry point, and the only model stated to work with an adjustable base.
  • Sunset Glow — from $1,599: the 14-inch Euro top and the plusher middle option. Two plush SmartFoam HD comfort layers in the Euro top, gel memory foam and an OrthoCoils+ pocketed coil system with 360° foam encasement.
  • Midnight Diamond — from $2,499: the 16-inch Euro top and the luxury flagship. Multiple plush comfort layers including a natural latex layer, OrthoCoils+ coils and a temperature-regulating design. The deepest cushioning of the three.

All three are made-to-order in the USA with globally sourced materials, and all three are sold only through the brand's official website. What the line does not yet have is just as important: no published sleep trial, no published warranty, no per-size prices and no independent reviews — the launch is four days old. This article gives you the verified construction and pricing, and flags everything that is still unpublished.

How to Choose: Which Bre Collection Mattress Fits You?

With no firmness numbers published, the choice comes down to height, top style and budget — and the three models are spaced widely enough that the decision is fairly clear.

Pick Golden Hour if you want the most balanced feel for the least money. At 12 inches with a tight top, it is the only model without a Euro top, and the brand calls it the most balanced of the three: gentle cushioning over responsive support. It is also the only one explicitly described as adjustable-base friendly, and at $999 it is the entry point into the line.

Pick Sunset Glow if you want a plusher, more cushioned surface without paying the luxury price. The 14-inch Euro top adds a second plush SmartFoam HD comfort layer and deeper cushioning, while the OrthoCoils+ system underneath keeps it supportive. At $1,599 it is $600 more than Golden Hour and $900 less than Midnight Diamond — the middle of the line in every sense.

Pick Midnight Diamond if you want the deepest cushioning and the most premium build. The 16-inch Euro top is the tallest and plushest of the three, and it is the only model with a natural latex layer in its comfort stack. At $2,499 it competes with established luxury mattresses — and unlike those, it has no published trial or warranty yet, which is the real trade-off.

Two practical limits apply to all three. Sizes are Queen, Eastern King and California King only, so there is no option for a Twin or Full bed. And because the mattresses are made-to-order, expect a production wait after ordering — the brand does not publish a delivery timeframe, so ask before you buy if timing matters.

Our Verdict: Who Should Buy It

Sunset Glow is the pick of the three — with a caveat. At $1,599 it is the middle model, and it is the one where the Euro-top cushioning actually changes the sleep feel rather than just adding height. Golden Hour is the sensible budget choice, but its tight top and thinner comfort stack make it the least differentiated model in the line. Midnight Diamond is the luxury statement, and its natural latex layer is genuinely interesting — but $2,499 without a published trial or warranty is a lot to ask of a four-day-old brand.

The caveat matters more than the ranking. Every established mattress in this price range comes with a published sleep trial (usually 100 to 365 nights) and a warranty (usually 10 years or more). The Bre Collection publishes neither as of November 9, 2026. That does not mean the terms are bad — it means they are unknown, and you should not spend $1,000 to $2,500 on a mattress without knowing them. Before buying, check the official site for trial and warranty terms, confirm the price for your size, and ask about made-to-order delivery times.

Who this line is really for: shoppers who like the California-luxury positioning, want a made-to-order US-built hybrid, and are comfortable buying a brand-new line on the strength of its construction and its manufacturer. If you want a proven track record, independent test scores or a long trial, the established brands in our best mattress of 2026 guide are the safer buy today — and the Bre Collection is worth revisiting once its trial terms are published and the first reviews appear.
