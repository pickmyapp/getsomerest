Plank Launches Three New Firm Mattresses: Essential vs RX vs Luxe Zoned Compared (2026)

Plank's three new firm mattresses — Essential from $1,332, Luxe Zoned from $1,865 and Firm Rx from $2,532 per Queen — compared: construction, cooling upgrades, trial and who each one suits.

By GetSome Rest editorial team · Independent sleep product guides Published Nov 9, 2026 · Last updated Nov 9, 2026

Plank, Brooklyn Bedding's extra-firm label, has replaced its firm-mattress lineup with three new models that launched in late October 2026: the Essential, the Luxe Zoned and the Firm Rx. All three are hybrids built around individually encased coils, all three carry a 365-night trial and a limited lifetime warranty, and all three are aimed at sleepers who want a firm surface — but they are built very differently. The Essential is a slim 9-inch value hybrid, the Luxe Zoned is a flippable 13.25-inch mattress with zoned lumbar support on both sides, and the Firm Rx is the premium build, with microcoils and a thick layer of high-density memory foam. Here is how they compare, what they cost, and which one fits which sleeper.

Quick answer

The short answer: Plank's three new firm mattresses start at $1,332 (Essential), $1,865 (Luxe Zoned) and $2,532 (Firm Rx) for a Queen with the standard cover. The Essential is the budget extra-firm pick, the flippable Luxe Zoned is the one built for side sleepers who still want firm support, and the Firm Rx is the most contouring of the three. All three include a 365-night trial and a limited lifetime warranty. No independent lab testing of the new models has been published yet — specs and prices below come from the manufacturer.

At a Glance: Plank's Three New Firm Mattresses

Plank has been Brooklyn Bedding's extra-firm label for years, and the three models that launched in late October 2026 are a clean-sheet replacement for the old Plank Firm and Plank Firm Luxe rather than a refresh. The launch was reported on October 29, 2026, and the three product pages went live on Plank's Shopify store on October 26 and 28 — so the beds are brand new, and no independent lab has published hands-on testing of any of them yet. Everything below comes from the manufacturer's own product pages, which is the honest basis for a launch comparison.

  • Plank Essential — from $1,332 (Queen): the value pick. A 9-inch single-surface hybrid with a 6-inch coil core, the thinnest comfort stack of the three, and the firmest feel. For budget-minded sleepers who want extra-firm support and nothing else.
  • Plank Luxe Zoned — from $1,865 (Queen): the versatile pick. A 13.25-inch flippable hybrid with zoned lumbar support on both sides — Firm on one side, Extra-Firm on the other — and the only one of the three Plank positions for side sleepers.
  • Plank Firm Rx — from $2,532 (Queen): the premium pick. A 13-inch hybrid with microcoils, a full inch of high-density memory foam and a foam-encased coil unit for edge-to-edge stability. The most contouring and pressure-relieving of the three, aimed at firm-feel sleepers who want more cushioning.

All three share the same trial and warranty — 365 nights and a limited lifetime warranty — and all three offer the optional GlacioTex cool-to-the-touch cover for $200 on a Queen. The Firm Rx alone adds a ThermoPhase phase-change foam upgrade for sleepers who run hot.

How to Choose: Which New Plank Fits You?

Start with how much cushioning you want, because that is the real dividing line between these three. Firm does not mean identical — the Essential barely sinks at all, the Luxe Zoned gives a little on its Firm side, and the Firm Rx is the only one with a meaningful memory-foam comfort layer.

Pick the Essential if you want the firmest surface for the least money and you sleep on your back or stomach. Its 9-inch build has just one inch of quilt foam and one inch of transition foam over the coil unit, so there is very little sink. It is also the lightest and simplest of the three, which matters if you move the mattress yourself or pair it with an adjustable base.

Pick the Luxe Zoned if you sleep on your side but still want a firm bed, or if your firmness preference changes with the seasons. The flippable design gives you two genuinely different feels — Firm and Extra-Firm — and the zoned lumbar support on both sides reinforces the centre third of the bed, which is where side sleepers' hips usually sink too far on a flat firm surface. It is the only one of the three endorsed by the American Chiropractic Association for its firm benefits, per the product page.

Pick the Firm Rx if you want firm support with real contouring. The microcoil layer and the inch of HD 3.6 memory foam give it more pressure relief than the other two, and the foam-encased coil perimeter holds the edges firm when you sit or sleep near them. It is the most expensive of the three by a wide margin, so it makes sense mainly if the Essential feels too hard and the Luxe Zoned feels too flat.

A note on firmness numbers: Plank has not published a 1-to-10 firmness rating, a weight range or a weight limit for any of the three new models. Numbers you may see on third-party pages — 7 to 9 out of 10, a 950 lb limit — belong to the older Plank Firm and Firm Luxe and do not apply here. Judge by the layer stacks and the brand's own positioning instead.

Cooling, in plain terms: none of the three claims active cooling. The optional GlacioTex cover is a cool-to-the-touch fabric upgrade, and the Firm Rx's ThermoPhase option is a phase-change foam that absorbs some heat. The coil cores themselves encourage airflow, which is the main cooling story for all three — a real but modest one. If you sleep very hot, the GlacioTex cover is the $200 decision to make, not the model choice.

Buying: all three are sold direct from Plank's own store. There is no Amazon listing for the new models yet — Amazon searches currently return only the older Plank Firm and Firm Luxe. If you do shop for them on Amazon, use a search such as Plank Essential on Amazon, Plank Luxe Zoned on Amazon or Plank Firm Rx on Amazon and check that the listing is the new model before buying.

Our Verdict: Who Should Buy Which

The Luxe Zoned is the pick for most firm-mattress shoppers. It costs $533 more than the Essential per Queen, but it buys the two things the Essential cannot offer: a flippable second feel and zoned lumbar support that makes a firm bed workable for side sleepers. For a mattress you will sleep on for years, that flexibility is worth more than the price gap, and it is the only one of the three with a credible side-sleeper story.

The Essential is the right buy if the budget is the deciding factor and you already know you like a very firm, flat surface — back and stomach sleepers who have slept happily on firm beds before. At $1,332 for a Queen with a 365-night trial, it is one of the cheapest ways into a US-assembled firm hybrid, and its simplicity is a feature, not a compromise, for that buyer.

The Firm Rx is the specialist. At $2,532 it is nearly double the Essential, and the extra money buys contouring and edge support rather than a different firmness. It makes sense for firm-feel sleepers who found past firm mattresses too hard on shoulders and hips, or who want the strongest edge support of the three. Everyone else can save the $667 over the Luxe Zoned.

One caution that applies to all three: there is no independent testing yet. The old Plank Firm Luxe scored well in lab reviews (Sleep Foundation's test-lab score was 8.7), but those results belong to a different mattress. Buy on the trial — 365 nights is generous — and use the 30-day minimum try-out the brand requires before deciding.

What Early Coverage Says

There is no independent hands-on testing of the Essential, RX or Luxe Zoned yet. Every review currently indexed — Sleep Foundation, Sleepopolis, Mattress Clarity, NapLab — covers the older Plank Firm or Plank Firm Luxe, and their scores must not be attached to the new models. The launch was reported on October 29, 2026, and the only coverage so far is the announcement itself plus spec summaries on roundup sites, some of which still carry the old models' terms (120-night trial, 10-year warranty) and wrong prices. Treat any third-party page that quotes those terms for the new models as stale.

What is verifiable is the manufacturer's own data: the three product pages list full layer stacks, the Shopify variant data confirms the prices in the table above, and Brooklyn Bedding's support pages confirm the 365-night trial, limited lifetime warranty and shipping terms. That is a solid basis for a launch comparison, and it is also the limit of what can honestly be said until test labs get units.

What to watch: the first independent reviews will matter most for the Luxe Zoned, because its flippable zoned design is the hardest claim to verify from a spec sheet — whether the two sides genuinely feel different, and whether the zoned lumbar support holds up for side sleepers. We will update this article when test data appears.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Plank's three new mattresses cost?

Queen prices with the standard cover are $1,332 for the Essential, $1,865 for the Luxe Zoned and $2,532 for the Firm Rx, as listed on Plank's own store on November 9, 2026. Twin sizes start at $899, $1,299 and $1,932, and Kings run $1,599, $2,199 and $3,032. A GlacioTex cooling cover adds $200 on a Queen for any of the three, and the Firm Rx also offers a ThermoPhase phase-change foam upgrade.

Which Plank is best for side sleepers?

The Luxe Zoned is the one Plank positions for side sleepers who want a firm mattress. It is flippable — a Firm side and an Extra-Firm side — and both sides have zoned lumbar support that reinforces the centre third of the bed under the hips and lower back. The Essential and the Firm Rx are single-surface mattresses, and the Essential is the firmest-feeling of the three at 9 inches with a thin comfort stack.

What is the sleep trial and warranty on the new Plank mattresses?

All three come with a 365-night sleep trial and a limited lifetime warranty. There is a 30-day minimum try-out after delivery before a return can be started, and the trial can be used once per mattress. Shipping is free FedEx Ground to the contiguous US, with 5 to 12 business days of production before 3 to 5 business days of delivery.

Are the new Plank mattresses on Amazon?

Not yet. As of November 9, 2026, no Amazon listing exists for the Essential, Luxe Zoned or Firm Rx — only the older Plank Firm and Plank Firm Luxe are sold there. The new models are sold direct from Plank's own store. Note that older Plank models bought on Amazon fall under Amazon's 30-night return policy, not Brooklyn Bedding's 365-night trial.

Is the Plank Luxe Zoned the same as the old Plank Firm Luxe?

No. The Luxe Zoned is a new model launched in late October 2026, with zoned lumbar support on both flippable sides and a 13.25-inch profile. The older Plank Firm Luxe is a different mattress with its own review history — its test scores and prices should not be attached to the new model, and no independent testing of the Luxe Zoned has been published yet.
