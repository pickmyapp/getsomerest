Stearns & Foster 2026 Luxury Mattress Collections Explained (2026)

At a Glance: The 2026 Stearns & Foster Collections

Stearns & Foster has made luxury innerspring mattresses in the US since 1846, and the 2026 line is a ground-up rebuild rather than a refresh. Four collections replace the old lineup, all built on the same platform — patented IntelliCoil innersprings with PrecisionEdge edge support, TEMPUR-Indulge memory foam in the comfort layers, and a new TrioTemp Cooling Cover — with hand-tufted covers and handcrafted construction throughout.

  • Estate — from $2,399 (Queen tight top): IntelliCoil and PrecisionEdge coils, TEMPUR-Indulge foam, TrioTemp Cooling Cover, four air vents. Hybrid Medium, Pillow Top Soft and Firm, and Tight Top Medium, Firm and Ultra Firm.
  • Lux Estate — from $2,999: everything in Estate, plus a Lux Memory Foam Quilt for extra pressure relief and eight air vents. Hybrid Soft and Firm, Pillow Top Soft and Medium, Tight Top Medium and Firm.
  • Legacy — from $3,999: adds the MicroTouch Support Layer, sustainably sourced latex and HeatDiffusing Fibers, with eight vents. The widest feel range in the line, from Hybrid Soft to Tight Top Ultra Firm.
  • Reserve — from $4,999: IntelliCoil HD with 20% more coils, MicroTouch layer, latex and HeatDiffusing Foams, eight vents. The flagship, including the Reserve Duet with a TENCEL and wool cover.
  • Availability: retailers since October 2026, direct-to-consumer at StearnsAndFoster.com from November 2026, phased rollout replacing the old line; some retailers carry current inventory through February 2027.
  • Warranty: 10-year limited warranty; 90-night trial on the brand site, retailer terms in stores.

The headline is the cooling: the TrioTemp cover and the vent counts are the first time Stearns & Foster has made temperature a headline feature across the whole line, and the TEMPUR-Indulge foam ties the brand directly to its parent company's best-known material. The question, as always with luxury mattresses, is how much of the ladder you need — and the rest of this article works through exactly that.

How to Choose: Which Collection Fits You?

Start with your budget, because the range is wide: $2,399 to $6,599 for a Queen, and the collections are spaced $600 to $1,000 apart. The Estate at $2,399 is the value play — you get the IntelliCoil springs, the TEMPUR-Indulge foam and the TrioTemp cover, which is most of what changed in 2026. If your budget tops out near $2,500, the Estate is the collection, and the right question is which feel: Hybrid Medium, Pillow Top Soft or Firm, or one of the Tight Top options.

Move to Lux Estate at $2,999 if pressure relief is the priority. The Lux Memory Foam Quilt is the one addition that changes how the bed feels rather than how it is built — extra conforming comfort on top of the same springs — and the vent count doubles from four to eight. Reviewers noticed: Sleepopolis rated the Lux Estate Medium Pillow Top 4.8 out of 5 in October 2026, calling it plush but supportive. For back sleepers who want cushioning without sinking, this is the sweet spot of the line.

Legacy at $3,999 is for sleepers who want natural materials in the mix: sustainably sourced latex and the MicroTouch Support Layer join the foam and springs, with HeatDiffusing Fibers added to the cooling package. Reserve at $4,999 is the flagship — IntelliCoil HD with 20% more coils, the MicroTouch layer, latex, HeatDiffusing Foams and hand-tufting throughout. The jump from Legacy to Reserve buys coil density and finishing, not a different feel, so try both before paying it.

Two practical checks before you buy. First, latex: Lux Estate, Legacy and Reserve contain latex, so if you have a severe latex allergy, the Estate is your collection. Second, the trial: the brand site lists a 90-night trial, but retailer terms are set by the retailer — confirm the trial length and any return fee in writing, since a $4,999 mattress with a restocking fee is a different purchase from one without.

Our Verdict: Which Collection to Buy

The 2026 rebuild is a genuine improvement, not a rebadge. The TrioTemp Cooling Cover on every collection, the TEMPUR-Indulge foam, and the vent doubling from Estate to Lux Estate address the two complaints luxury innersprings have carried for years — sleeping warm, and feeling generic next to the direct-to-consumer brands. The line finally has a clear ladder, and each step buys something specific.

The case against the top of the line is value. The Reserve at $4,999-plus is a beautiful bed, but the step from Legacy buys coil density and finishing rather than a different night's sleep, and independent reviewers have noted that the Reserve line adds cost for incremental, not transformational, improvement. The Estate at $2,399 is arguably the best value in the line — most of the 2026 changes for the lowest price — and the Lux Estate at $2,999 is the pick if you want the quilt and the eight vents.

Our take: buy the Lux Estate if you want the best balance of the new line — the quilt, the eight vents, the 4.8-out-of-5 review, and a price that stays under $3,000 at list. Buy the Estate if your budget is firm and you want the new platform for the least money. Buy Legacy or Reserve only after lying on both in a store, because at $3,999 and $4,999 you are paying for materials you should feel before you fund. And whatever you buy, confirm the trial terms with the actual seller — they are not the same everywhere.

Coverage so far is the announcement plus first reviews. The PR Newswire release of July 22, 2026 sets out the whole line: four redesigned collections, launching at retailers in October and direct-to-consumer in November, ranging from $2,399 to $6,599, with the phased rollout replacing the existing lineup while select partners carry current inventory through February 2027.

The first hands-on data point is Sleepopolis, which rated the Lux Estate Medium Pillow Top 4.8 out of 5 on October 6, 2026, describing it as plush but supportive. Retailer guides agree on the shape of the line: DeWaard & Bode calls the Estate the best value, the Lux Estate the top pick for back support, and the Reserve the most luxurious, and lists the Estate from around $2,199 at retail against the $2,399 list price.

Independent roundups add two cautions worth knowing. First, prices vary by seller — retailer queens run a few hundred below list, and sale pricing moves often, so the list price is a ceiling, not the price you will pay. Second, trial and return terms depend on where you buy: the brand site lists a 90-night trial, but retailer terms are retailer-set, including return fees. Both cautions point the same way: buy from a seller whose trial terms you have in writing.

Frequently asked questions

How much do the 2026 Stearns & Foster mattresses cost?

The range runs $2,399 to $6,599. By collection, a Queen tight top starts at $2,399 for Estate, $2,999 for Lux Estate, $3,999 for Legacy and $4,999 for Reserve. Those are the brand's list prices; retailers discount — Sleep Center Direct, for example, lists queens from $2,199, $2,599, $3,399 and $4,199. Prices are as listed on November 8, 2026, and foundations and bases are sold separately.

What is the difference between Estate, Lux Estate, Legacy and Reserve?

The step-ups are the coils, the comfort layers and the cooling. Estate has patented IntelliCoil innersprings with PrecisionEdge coils, TEMPUR-Indulge memory foam, the TrioTemp Cooling Cover and four air vents. Lux Estate adds a Lux Memory Foam Quilt and eight air vents. Legacy adds a MicroTouch Support Layer, sustainably sourced latex and HeatDiffusing Fibers. Reserve moves to IntelliCoil HD — 20% more coils — with the MicroTouch layer, latex and HeatDiffusing Foams. All four are handcrafted with hand-tufted covers.

Where can I buy the 2026 Stearns & Foster collections?

At premium retailers nationwide since October 2026, and direct-to-consumer at StearnsAndFoster.com from November 2026. The rollout is phased: the new collections replace the existing lineup gradually, and select retail partners will keep carrying current inventory through February 2027 — so if a store still shows the old line, it is not a mistake, it is the transition.

Do the 2026 Stearns & Foster mattresses sleep cool?

Cooling is the headline change across the line. Every collection gets the new TrioTemp Cooling Cover, and the vent count doubles from four on the Estate to eight on the Lux Estate, Legacy and Reserve. Legacy and Reserve add HeatDiffusing Fibers or Foams to move heat away from the surface. That is a real step up from the old line — but these are still innerspring hybrids with foam comfort layers, not active-cooling beds, so if you sleep very hot, compare them with an actively cooled system before deciding.

What is the trial and warranty?

Stearns & Foster mattresses carry a 10-year limited warranty. The trial depends on where you buy: the brand site lists a 90-night trial, while retailer terms are set by the retailer, including return fees. If you buy through a store, confirm the trial length and the return cost in writing before ordering — and note the latex: Lux Estate, Legacy and Reserve contain latex, so skip them if you have a severe latex allergy.
