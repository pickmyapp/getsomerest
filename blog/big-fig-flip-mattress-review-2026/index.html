Big Fig Flip Mattress Review (2026): Two Mattresses in One

At a Glance: Two Mattresses in One

Big Fig has always been the mattress for people other brands forget: heavier sleepers whose weight breaks down standard beds. The Flip, launched in mid-October 2026, keeps that mission and adds something the original never had — a second opinion. It is a 13-inch, two-sided hybrid with a plush side and a firm side, so the same mattress can be soft or firm depending on which way up it lies.

  • Plush side — 5/10: the pressure-relieving side, with more give under shoulders and hips. The starting side for side sleepers and anyone coming from a softer bed.
  • Firm side — 8/10: the supportive side, with a flatter, more solid feel. The starting side for back and stomach sleepers, and for heavier sleepers who need the bed to hold its shape.
  • Built for weight: rated to support up to 1,200 lbs, with individually wrapped steel coils under foam comfort layers on both sides.
  • Cooling on both sides: LDPE and ThermoGel cooling technology on the plush and firm sides alike.
  • Queen $2,399, or $1,899 with code: the current Black Friday code FRIYAY takes $500 off any mattress, including the Flip. Prices run $1,949 (Twin) to $3,449 (Split King) before discounts.
  • 120-night trial, lifetime warranty: the same terms as the original Big Fig — and the protection that matters most, since no independent testing of the Flip exists yet.

The Flip is not a refresh of the original Big Fig. It is a different bed with a different buyer in mind: the heavy sleeper who is not sure how firm they want to be, and who would rather flip a mattress than replace one.

How to Choose: Which Side Fits You?

Start with the side you will sleep on first, because that is the decision the Flip is really selling. The two sides are not subtle variations — 5/10 and 8/10 are three points apart on a ten-point scale, which in mattress terms is the difference between a bed you sink into and a bed you sleep on top of.

Start on the plush side (5/10) if you sleep on your side, or if pressure on your shoulders and hips is what wakes you up. The plush side is the pressure-relieving side: more give under the shoulders and hips, which is what side sleepers need to keep their spine level. It is also the right starting side if you are coming from a softer mattress and are not sure you want Big Fig's famously firm feel — the plush side is the gentlest bed the brand has ever made.

Start on the firm side (8/10) if you sleep on your back or stomach, or if you are a heavier sleeper who has watched softer mattresses sag. The firm side is the support side: less sink, a flatter surface, and the feel closest to the original Big Fig's 9/10. Back sleepers who want their hips held level and stomach sleepers who need their midsection supported will find it here.

Flip when something changes. That is the design's real argument: bodies change, injuries happen, preferences drift, and a mattress that can be flipped is a mattress you do not have to replace when they do. Two sleepers who disagree can also trade sides without buying a split mattress — though flipping a 13-inch hybrid is a two-person job, and worth planning for.

A note on the 1,200-lb capacity: Big Fig rates the Flip to support up to 1,200 lbs, the same figure it uses for the original Big Fig. That is a weight capacity for the mattress, not a recommendation — and it is a manufacturer figure, not an independently tested one. What it signals is the target buyer: heavier sleepers, couples whose combined weight stresses a standard mattress, and anyone who has worn out a conventional bed early. If you are under 200 lbs and sleep alone, the Flip's capacity is not a reason to buy it; the two-sided design is.

Cooling, in plain terms: both sides of the Flip carry LDPE and ThermoGel cooling technology, so flipping the mattress changes the feel, not the temperature story. ThermoGel is Big Fig's cool-to-the-touch fabric treatment, and the hybrid coil core encourages airflow. There is no active cooling here — no fans, no water, no phase-change upgrade — so very hot sleepers should treat the cooling as a modest bonus, not the reason to buy.

Buying: the Flip is sold direct from Big Fig's own store. There is no Amazon listing for the Flip — Amazon searches return the original Big Fig and other brands instead. If you do shop for Big Fig on Amazon, use a search such as Big Fig mattress on Amazon and check that the listing is the model you want before buying.

Our Verdict: Who Should Buy Which

The Flip is the pick for heavy sleepers who are not sure how firm they want to be. That is a narrow sentence, but it is the honest one. If you weigh over 250 lbs, or you share a bed with a combined weight that has killed mattresses before, and you have ever bought a firm mattress and regretted it — or a soft one and regretted it — the Flip's two sides solve a real problem no single-sided mattress can: you get to change your mind without buying a second bed. At $1,899 for a Queen with the current code, it costs $50 more than the original Big Fig's sale Queen price of $1,849, and that $50 buys the second side.

Buy the original Big Fig instead if you already know you want very firm. The original is 9/10, one-sided, 11.5 inches, and cheaper in every size — Queen $1,849 on sale against the Flip's $1,899 with code. If you have slept happily on a firm Big Fig before, the Flip's plush side is a feature you will never use, and the original's lower price and longer track record (including independent testing) are worth more than a second feel.

Consider the Layla Hybrid instead if you are under 200 lbs and want a flippable mattress at a lower price. The Layla Hybrid is also two-sided — 4/10 soft, 7/10 firm — with a Queen at $1,699, and it has years of independent reviews behind it. It is not built for heavy sleepers the way the Flip is, and its firm side is softer than the Flip's, but for an average-weight sleeper who wants two feels, it is the cheaper, better-tested option.

One caution that applies to the Flip specifically: there is no independent testing yet. The firmness numbers, the capacity figure and the cooling claims are all Big Fig's own. The 120-night trial is the protection — use it, sleep on both sides for at least a few weeks each, and decide with your own body rather than the spec sheet.

What Early Coverage Says

There is no independent hands-on testing of the Flip yet. The mattress launched in mid-October 2026, and as of November 10, 2026 the only coverage is the launch announcement and spec summaries. Chubstr's overview of October 15, 2026 is the most complete: it confirms the two-sided design, the 5/10 and 8/10 ratings, the 13-inch profile, the 1,200-lb capacity, the 120-night trial and the lifetime warranty, and it lists launch pricing by size with the BIGFLIPNDEAL code ($400 off, Queen $1,999). That code has since been superseded by the Black Friday FRIYAY code ($500 off, Queen $1,899), which is the price quoted in this article.

What is verifiable is the manufacturer's own data: the product pages list both sides' firmness ratings, the cooling technology on each side, the full size range and prices, and the trial and warranty terms. The brand has not published a full layer-by-layer construction breakdown or a coil count for the Flip — the detailed layer stack sometimes quoted for Big Fig (ThermoGel fabric, gel-infused latex, three layers of high-density poly foam, 1,600 individually wrapped coils) belongs to the original Big Fig mattress, not the Flip, and should not be attached to it.

What to watch: the first independent reviews will matter most for the plush side, because it is the claim that is hardest to verify from a spec sheet — whether a mattress built for 1,200 lbs can genuinely feel soft at 5/10, and whether the two sides feel as different in practice as the numbers suggest. We will update this article when test data appears.

Frequently asked questions

How much does the Big Fig Flip cost?

List prices run from $1,949 (Twin) to $3,449 (Split King), with a Queen at $2,399 and a King at $2,599. As of November 10, 2026, Big Fig's Black Friday sale takes $500 off any mattress with code FRIYAY — a Queen drops to $1,899, a Full to $1,699, a Twin XL to $1,599 and a Split King to $2,949. The launch code BIGFLIPNDEAL ($400 off, Queen $1,999) has been superseded by the Black Friday code. Prices are from Big Fig's own store and can change; check the product page before buying.

What is the difference between the plush and firm sides?

The plush side is rated 5/10 on Big Fig's firmness scale — soft, pressure-relieving comfort for side sleepers and anyone who wants more give. The firm side is rated 8/10 — a solid, supportive surface for back and stomach sleepers and for heavier sleepers who need the bed to hold its shape. Both sides use the same cooling technology (LDPE and ThermoGel), so flipping changes the feel, not the temperature story. The mattress is 13 inches thick and rated to support up to 1,200 lbs.

How is the Flip different from the original Big Fig mattress?

The original Big Fig is a one-sided, 11.5-inch mattress rated 9/10 — very firm, built for heavier sleepers, with a Queen at $1,849 on sale. The Flip is a different bed: two-sided, 13 inches thick, with a genuinely soft side (5/10) the original never offered. If you already know you want very firm, the original is cheaper; if your firmness preference might change, or two sleepers disagree, the Flip's second side is the reason it exists.

What is the sleep trial and warranty on the Flip?

The Flip comes with a 120-night sleep trial and a lifetime warranty, matching the original Big Fig. Big Fig sells direct from its own store; there is no Amazon listing for the Flip, and the brand does not sell through third-party retailers for this model. Returns go through Big Fig's own process, so read the trial terms on the product page before ordering — including any return shipping or restocking terms.

Has anyone independently tested the Big Fig Flip?

Not yet. The Flip launched in mid-October 2026, and as of November 10, 2026 no independent lab or review site has published hands-on testing of it. The only coverage so far is the launch announcement and spec summaries, notably Chubstr's October 15, 2026 overview. Firmness ratings (5/10 and 8/10) and the 1,200-lb capacity are the manufacturer's own figures. Buy on the 120-night trial and judge the two sides yourself.
