How to Read a Black Friday Mattress Deal

Mattress marketing is built on discounts, and Black Friday is its biggest stage. A few rules keep the season honest.

Most brands discount year-round. Nectar, DreamCloud, Helix and Bear run a sale in most weeks of the year. That does not make Black Friday fake — the discounts are often the deepest of the year — but it does mean the gap between a Black Friday price and an ordinary Tuesday price is usually tens or low hundreds of dollars, not the thousand-dollar savings the struck-through price suggests. Judge a deal against what the bed cost last month, not against the list price.

Watch the struck-through price. Some brands publish no list price at all — DreamCloud and Nectar are the notable examples — which makes a '60% off' claim impossible to check against their own history. A queen at $699 is a good price whatever the percentage says; a percentage without a verifiable 'was' price is just advertising.

Mattress-only versus bundle. Several of the biggest percentages this year are bundle deals: Nectar offers up to 66% off bundles, DreamCloud up to 66% on bundles with $599 of accessories included. A bundle can be good value if you need the base and bedding, and a trap if you do not. Compare the mattress-only price first, then decide whether the extras are worth the difference.

Trial and warranty are part of the price. A $699 mattress with a 365-night trial and free returns is a safer buy than a $649 mattress with a 30-night break-in and a return fee. We list trial terms for every deal below, because the cheapest mattress that you cannot return is not the cheapest mattress.

Check delivery before the holiday. Black Friday is the busiest shipping week of the year. Most brands ship free in the contiguous US, but windows stretch, and old-mattress removal is usually a paid extra — Saatva includes white-glove delivery and removal on many orders.

What the Fine Print Says

The deal is only half the purchase. These are the terms that decide whether a cheap mattress stays cheap.

Break-in periods. Nectar and Bear require 30 nights before you can start a return; Purple requires 21. That is not a trick — a mattress needs weeks for your body to adjust — but it means you cannot buy on Black Friday and return on Cyber Monday.

Return fees. Most brands in this guide return for free. Saatva is the exception: a $99 pickup fee applies if you return. On a $1,904 mattress that is about 5% of the price, and worth knowing before you order.

Trial lengths. Nectar, DreamCloud, Saatva and Plank all offer 365 nights. Helix and Big Fig offer 120 nights; Bear offers 120 nights with a 30-night wait; Purple offers 100 nights. A year-long trial is the industry's best protection against a bad fit, and it costs you nothing to have it.

Warranties. Nectar and DreamCloud carry a Forever Warranty; Saatva and Plank carry lifetime warranties; Helix carries a limited lifetime warranty; Purple carries 10 years. Warranty length is a weak signal on its own — read what it covers, especially sagging depth — but a 10-year minimum is the floor you should accept.

Delivery and removal. Bed-in-a-box brands ship free and compressed in a box; you unbox and wait for it to expand. Saatva delivers white-glove, with setup and old-mattress removal. If you are replacing a heavy old mattress and live upstairs, that service is worth real money.

Price changes after you buy. Most brands will honour a lower price within a short window after purchase — typically 30 days — but the policy varies and is rarely advertised. If a price drops the week after you order, it is worth asking; the worst they can say is no.

Frequently asked questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday follows on November 30. Most mattress brands run their sales as a window rather than a single day: our tracking window is November 23 to December 1, and some offers run longer — Purple's mattress-and-base deal runs through December 7, and Serta and Beautyrest's in-store set offers run November 3 to December 1.

Will mattress prices drop further on Black Friday itself?

Sometimes, but not reliably. Several brands hold one price for the whole sale window, and a few deepen the discount on the day itself. The honest pattern from past years: the best price of the season usually appears somewhere inside the Black Friday window, but it is rarely much lower than the early-sale price — and many of these brands discount year-round, so a Black Friday price is often close to an ordinary sale price. If a bed is already at the price you want, waiting for Friday itself rarely pays.

Which Black Friday mattress deal is the best overall?

It depends on budget. Under $750, the Nectar Classic and DreamCloud Classic Hybrid at $699 a queen are the strongest value, both with 365-night trials. In the mid-range, the Helix Midnight at $1,183 with 20% off is the best-tested pick of the group. For premium buyers, the Saatva Classic at $1,904 is the most established luxury option. Heavy sleepers should look at the Big Fig Flip at $1,899 with code FRIYAY, and firm-mattress fans at the Plank Essential at about $932 with code VETERANS30.

Do sleep trials and warranties still apply during Black Friday sales?

Yes — sale mattresses carry the same trial and warranty terms as full-price ones, and that is part of what makes the deals safe. Nectar and DreamCloud offer 365-night trials with free returns; Helix offers 120 nights; Saatva offers 365 nights but charges a $99 pickup fee for returns; Purple offers 100 nights; Big Fig and Plank offer 120 and 365 nights respectively. Check the break-in period before returning: Nectar and Bear both require 30 nights before a return, and Purple requires 21.

Can I stack discount codes on a Black Friday mattress?

No, in almost every case. Mattress brands accept one code per order, and sale prices already reflect the deepest code. Military, student, first-responder and healthcare discounts also do not stack with Black Friday codes — Plank, for example, states that its VETERANS30 code cannot be combined with other promotional codes. If you qualify for a standing discount, compare it against the sale price and take whichever is lower.
